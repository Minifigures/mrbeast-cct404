RULES + GAME THEORY
1. Hide

The hider places their deed in Briefcase A or B. An X goes in the other.

2. Pick

The picker chooses a briefcase to steal. If they find the deed, they take it.

3. Swap

Roles swap each round. Round 1: P1 hides, P2 picks. Round 2: P2 hides, P1 picks. First to 2 deeds wins. Tied? Sudden death.

Game Theory

This is a zero-sum, simultaneous-move game. The Nash Equilibrium is to randomize 50/50, meaning no pure strategy exists. The hider gains nothing by always choosing A, and the picker gains nothing by always choosing B. Both players' optimal play is a coin flip.

This is exactly what Beast Games exploits: the challenge looks like a skill game, but is fundamentally a coin flip dressed in spectacle. The production designs the system to create dramatic tension from what is, strategically, pure chance.
